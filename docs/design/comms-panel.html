BATC Comms Panel

A clickable sketch of what the pill could do if it took over the BeyondATC toolbar: ATC lines as captions, answers and requests one hover away, the flight's frequencies and the radio log in a panel. Exchanges are scripted from captured BeyondATC traffic. Hover the pill, click it, and use the buttons under the screen.

Scenario

Each scenario resets the radio. From "Parked", hover the pill to request a clearance, push and taxi, and walk a whole departure.

How it fits together

Three levels, one anchor

  • Glance: the pill, unchanged. It stays the status line and the title of everything below it.
  • Read: captions under the pill. The lines of an exchange stay until it closes, then leave together about 5 s later; the instruction ATC waits on is outlined until you answer. Other aircraft's lines leave one by one. "Mine" drops lines to other aircraft.
  • Answer: hover ATC's message or the pill and the answers slide out as a row of chips below the message, outside its box. They show in one place only: beside the pill when the message isn't on screen (captions off), with the Auto Respond / Auto Tune switches at the far end. The obvious one filled amber, up to three, "+N" shows the rest in place. No Say Again: the message is there to read again. At rest only the message shows.
  • Request: with nothing pending, hovering the pill offers BeyondATC's requests in the same row, in its own order (the likely next one first), three at a time and "+N" for the rest.
  • Look up: clicking the pill opens a panel with Frequencies (Departure / Arrival) and Log. It opens on Frequencies while ATC's handoff is pending, else on the tab used last. Settings sit behind the gear.

Decisions to make

  • Click = panel, settings behind a gear. Today a click opens the settings card. Settings are visited once; comms are used every few minutes.
  • The panel is non-modal. The settings card closes on outside clicks through a full-screen shade, which also swallows clicks meant for the cockpit. A panel left open during a flight can't do that, so it closes only with its ✕ or a click on the pill.
  • No scrolling. Wheel events in mainUi are unverified; mousemove and clicks are. The log pages with "Show earlier", lists are short enough to fit.
  • Buttons go quiet while the radio is busy. BeyondATC queues requests, but a reply clicked while ATC is still talking reads as a mistake. Greyed rows say "wait".
  • Auto Respond on: the tray and the panel still show the replies (you can answer first), with a note that BeyondATC will answer.

Sim constraints carried over

  • Every clickable element needs data-input-group (as the settings card does today); hover comes from mousemove positions.
  • Roboto only; no gap, color-mix or backdrop-filter. The sim-side CSS in this page follows that.
  • No keyboard or gamepad focus in mainUi: mouse (and VR laser) only, so targets are at least 26 px at 1080p.

Before building

  • One sniff with the toolbar open, clicking its Frequencies and Log tabs, a frequency and a reply, to capture the request/answer pairs.
  • The AGENTS.md rule allowing only the two switch commands widens to set_action, set_frequency, frequencies and atc_log, always on a user's click.
  • Check that a wheel event reaches mainUi; if it does, the log can scroll instead of paging.
  • Check that a right-click reaches mainUi (contextmenu or mousedown button 2) for COM2.

Suggested order

  • Captions and the log: read-only, nothing new on the socket.
  • Reply tray: one new command, the biggest gain in the cockpit.
  • Panel with requests; then Frequencies once its payload is known.

New settings

  • Captions: Off / Mine / All, and how long they stay.
  • AI traffic (exists) also filters captions and the log.
